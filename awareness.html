Cybersecurity Awareness

Learn how to recognize phishing attacks and protect yourself online.

Never Click Unknown Links

Avoid clicking links from unknown emails or text messages. Always verify before opening.

Check Sender Email

Fake email addresses often resemble real companies. Inspect the sender carefully.

Verify Domains

Always verify website URLs before entering passwords or banking details.

Enable 2FA

Two-factor authentication adds an extra security layer to your accounts.

Use Spam Filters

Enable spam filters to automatically detect suspicious emails.

Report Phishing

Report phishing emails to your organization or email provider immediately.

About PhishGuard

PhishGuard is an AI-driven cybersecurity platform designed to protect users against phishing attacks. It analyzes suspicious URLs, detects malicious websites, educates users through awareness modules, and promotes safe browsing practices.

Our mission is to make cybersecurity accessible to everyone, helping individuals and organizations stay secure in today's digital world.
